**Owner:** Valentina · **Epic:** E7 — Reports, indicators and QA · **Priority:** Media

> HU-39: As an advisor, I want to see my % of the weekly goal.
> HU-40: As a director, I want indicators by advisor and by zone.

## What was implemented

New module `src/modules/indicators/` (registered in `AppModule`), read-only.

### HU-39 — `GET /indicators/weekly-goal?date=YYYY-MM-DD`

For the authenticated advisor and the Monday–Sunday week of `date` (default today):

```json
{ "weekStart": "...", "weekEnd": "...", "goal": 12, "goalSource": "advisor", "scheduled": 6, "completed": 6, "percentage": 50 }
```

The goal is the advisor's `weeklyVisitGoal` (set by an admin in `PATCH /advisors/:id`, HU-07). Without one, it is the number of visits scheduled for the week (`goalSource: "scheduled"`). `percentage` = completed / goal, capped at 100.

### HU-40 — `GET /indicators/by-advisor` and `GET /indicators/by-zone`

Query: `from`, `to` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, default the current month), `zoneId`. Admins and regional directors; a director always gets their zone. Each row has:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `scheduledVisits` | visits scheduled in the range |
| `completedVisits` | of those, `VISITED` |
| `effectiveness` | completed / scheduled, %, one decimal |
| `quotesCount`, `quotedValue` | quotes created in the range and their total |
| `ordersCount`, `orderedValue` | orders created in the range, excluding cancelled |

By advisor groups by the visit/quote/order advisor (`advisor_id`); by zone groups by the client's zone. The zone filter of `by-advisor` uses the advisor's profile zone.
