# HU-06 — Assign roles and restrict what each one sees

**Owner:** Efrain · **Epic:** E1 — Authentication, roles and advisors · **Priority:** Alta
**Source:** `docs/Comercial360_Historias_y_Reparto_v2.pdf`

> As an administrator, I want to assign roles (Admin, Regional Director, Advisor), to restrict what each one sees.

## Acceptance criteria

| Criterion | Status |
|---|---|
| `@Roles` decorator | ✅ Done (already existed: `@Roles` + `RolesGuard`, `PATCH /auth/users/:userId/role`) |
| The advisor only sees their routes, visits and quotes | ✅ Done |
| The director sees their zone | ✅ Done |

## What was implemented

### 1. `CurrentActorProvider` (`src/shared/auth/`)

New port next to `CurrentAdvisorProvider`. `SessionCurrentActorProvider` reads the user id from the session and re-reads `role` and `zoneId` from the database (like `RolesGuard`), so a stale session can't widen access. The helper `scopeForActor(actor)` turns the role into a filter:

| Role | Scope |
|---|---|
| `ASESOR` | `{ advisorId: actor.id }` |
| `DIRECTOR_REGIONAL` | `{ zoneId: actor.zoneId }` (`-1` if they have none, so nothing leaks) |
| `ADMIN` | no restriction |

### 2. Where the scope applies

| Resource | Advisor | Director |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /routes`, `GET /routes/:id` | only routes assigned to them (others: 404) | their zone (already existed) |
| `GET /visits/agenda`, `/visits/:id` | only their visits (already existed) | — |
| `GET /quotes`, `GET /quotes/:id` | only quotes they created | quotes of clients in their zone |
| `GET /orders`, `GET /orders/:id` | only orders of their quotes | orders of clients in their zone |
| `GET /clients` | all clients (they quote and visit across zones) | clients of their zone |
| `GET /advisors`, reports, indicators | no access | their zone |

A record outside the scope is reported as 404, not 403, so its existence isn't revealed.

### 3. Advisor on quotes and orders

To know whose quote it is, `quotes` got `advisor_id` (the user who creates it, taken from the session) and `orders` got `advisor_id` (copied from the quote on conversion). Migrations: `quotes/.../1792720000200-AddQuoteAdvisorAndVisit.ts` and `orders/.../1792720000300-AddOrderAdvisor.ts`. Quotes created before the migration keep a `NULL` advisor, so only admins and directors see them. This also adds the missing `advisorId` filter of HU-34 to `GET /quotes` and `GET /orders`, and `GET /orders` now applies its `status` filter.

## Not covered

Write operations on quotes and orders (change status, attach, convert) are not limited by owner yet; only reads are.
